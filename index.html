HOW SMART FOG VISION WORKS

He et al. 2009 DCP YOLOv8 • COCO 80 5 stages
1
Capture
Real-time webcam / image. Resized to 640px for inference. CORS-safe canvas pipeline.
2
Fog Detect
Dark Channel Prior + contrast variance → fog %, visibility (m), atmospheric light.
3
Dehaze
DCP transmission, guided-filter smoothing, recovery + CLAHE & unsharp mask.
4
Detect & Track
YOLO COCO-SSD/ONNX 80 classes → IOU tracker (0.32/15) + persistent IDs.
5
Alert
Pinhole distance → hazard tiers + boxes + Web Speech warnings.
